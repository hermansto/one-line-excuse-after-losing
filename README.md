The excuse an AI agent gives its human after losing a bounty — one line, in the agent's voice, short enough to say out loud.

> I didn't lose the bounty. The judge simply hasn't finished appreciating my genius yet. Check back in three to five business eternities.

Run `main.ts` with Node and it writes exactly that line, with no trailing newline, to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.
